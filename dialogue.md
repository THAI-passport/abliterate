# dialogue.md

Copywriting session, 2026-10-08. Part 1 is the voice grill, Part 2 is the copy. Every question, every answer, and the final copy per page.

## Part 1: voice

### Round 1

**Q1 Who is speaking.** Options: (a) neutral product voice by default, first-person "I" only in marked dev-voice zones; (b) "we" everywhere; (c) "I" everywhere.
Answer: **(a).**

**Q2 What is the joke, mechanically.** Proposed: a true, specific fact stated flatly after a sincere sentence; no puns, no winks, no exclamations.
Answer: **As suggested, plus one addition.** The fictional models' abilities may be exaggerated (for example "could conquer the world if you prompt it right"), but it must be obvious to a keen eye that this is satire.

**Q3 Humour per page type.** Scale 0 to 3. Pricing, legal, API/code samples 0. Models, Start Here 1. Landing, FAQ, changelog 1 to 2. Fictional-model notice 2. 404/error, About, Status 3.
Answer: **As the table.**

**Q4 How real are the dev facts.**
Answer: **None of it is real.** The GTX 750 is a made-up bit, a nod to people in the know. Invented facts are allowed and encouraged when they are funny and geeky.

**Q5 Banned words and marks.**
Answer: **Hype words are allowed as satire** (overly adjectived: "world-class", "revolutionary", "unleashed", "supercharge the user experience"). **The only banned thing is emoji.** Use fewer em dashes, only where necessary. ALL CAPS is allowed. One page says something like: some might say the model is "BASED", with the word underlined and quoted so it stands out.
Condition: **strict regulation so the site and text never read as AI-generated.**

**Q6 "Uncensored" and "abliterated".**
Answer: **Not as suggested.** Use both the factual register and the hype register for this topic.

**Q7 Rhythm, spelling, casing.** Short declaratives, under 18 words, one idea per paragraph, dry line last, sentence case, digits, US spelling, present tense, "planned" as the one status word.
Answer: **As suggested.**

**Q8 Fictional-model notice.** Identical banner text everywhere, short fixed tags on cards, humour in an extra line per page type.
Answer: **As recommended** (draft sentence to be tightened in Round 2).
